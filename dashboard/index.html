The model — radar-5, one pooled tilt plus an out-of-fold calibration

implied = calibrate(TVL × fitted multiple × growth × maturity × chain reach), where fitted multiple = category multiple × size tilt

  • Category multiple — median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (3+ peers), else the whole-market median; clamped to 0.02–1.00.
  • Size tilt — one pooled fit, not one per category. Log(market cap) regressed on log(TVL) over all 119 live-token protocols at once (CEX excluded); slope 0.47, R² 0.14. The tilt is 1 at the market's median live TVL ($27.3M) and is applied at half the fitted strength, clamped to 0.50–1.50, because the fit is weak and a full-strength tilt would price the giants 8× below their own category's peers, an extrapolation, not a measurement. A bigger protocol usually trades at a lower multiple.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +100%) × 0.6.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.30; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain reach — 1 + min(chains − 1, 5) × 0.02.
  • Calibration — out-of-fold shrinkage on the full formula. The raw formula's errors are worst in the tails: its median error barely moved against the flat median while its trimmed mean fell hard, which says the middle is fine and the extremes are wrong. So a winsorized log-space line, fitted only on out-of-fold predictions of the 119 live peers, maps log(raw) to log(calibrated) and pulls the extremes back toward the truth. The map is monotone, the shift for any one protocol is capped at ±0.75 decades (about 5.6×), and the numbers below are the honest nested measurement — each fold's map fitted without that fold's peers — not the fitted layer's view of its own training data.

Fitting the calibration…

Why one pooled fit: per-category fits were run and rejected on the evidence — with 5 to 15 live peers per category they lose to the pooled fit on five of the six validation checks (see the table below). Fitting one slope per category sounds more precise; on 119 tokens it is mostly noise wearing a slope. The within-category slope was run too and ties the pooled fit, so it stays out for no gain.

No protocol name is special-cased. The calibration table below is recomputed from whatever snapshot the program is handed, so the radar keeps working on protocols that do not exist yet.
